A few pictures from the Côte d'Opale, north of France. 
Wide beaches under cloudy skies, kitesurfers out on the water, and cows in the countryside.

![A seaside house above a rocky sea wall and sandy beach](/blog/cote-d-opale-2021/IMG_7174.jpg)

I can't tell why I like this picture but I do, that house feels very lonely, like it was just put here randomly by someone.

![Shallow channels of water across a wide beach beneath grey clouds](/blog/cote-d-opale-2021/IMG_7210.jpg)

Reflections of the water on the sand, and a kite surf in the distance. 

![Kitesurfers riding near the shore under a cloudy sky](/blog/cote-d-opale-2021/IMG_7238.jpg)

Two people kite surfing, I liked the symetry. 

![Cows and calves standing in a green hillside pasture](/blog/cote-d-opale-2021/IMG_7378.jpg)

Cows in a prairie, I liked the look the white cow is giving us.
