A selection of pictures from Marseille. Reflections at the Vieux-Port, strong shadows, and views of the coast as the light changes.

![People walking beneath the mirrored canopy at Marseille's Vieux-Port in black and white](/blog/marseille-2022/14072015-IMG_3155.jpg)

On the vieux port of Marseille, this huge mirror called "l'ombrelle" was installed a couple of years ago. Using an ND filter, people look like shadows at the foreground, but their reflections in the mirror are less blurry. 

![The towers of Marseille Cathedral above dark rooftops at sunset](/blog/marseille-2022/L1010439.jpg)

Cathedrale de la Major, with beautiful sunset light.

![Notre-Dame de la Garde rising above a wooded hill beneath a blue sky](/blog/marseille-2022/L1010453.jpg)

Notre Dame de la Garde, or "bonne mere".

![Lattice shadows falling across a terrace beside a cafe](/blog/marseille-2022/L1010522.jpg)

Mucem museum, I like the shade and asymetry effect it gives.

![An overhead view of a pedestrian and a long shadow on paving stones](/blog/marseille-2022/L1010631.jpg)

Playing with the mirror "l'ombrelle" on the vieux port and this man's shadow.

![Sunbathers along a rocky waterfront beside turquoise water](/blog/marseille-2022/L1010646.jpg)

Plage des catalans and the famous "cercle des nageurs" where profesional swimming atheletes train. I like the vibrant colors on that summer day.

![A sailboat beside a rocky island beneath a peach-coloured sunset sky](/blog/marseille-2022/L1010731.jpg)

Iles du Frioul at sunset, old sailing boat with warm colors.
