A selection of pictures from the USA that I took during a 6 months internship in California. 
Yosemite's granite cliffs, wildlife, a quiet interior, and scenes from the California coast.

![A person looking up at a curved vapour trail in a clear sky in black and white](/blog/usa-2014/20141013-IMG_0985.jpg)

This is probably my favorite picture. Shot during fleet week in San Francisco. Funnily enough I completely forgot about this picture for years until I found it in my Lightroom, converted to black & white, cropped to 1:1 ratio, and voilà.

![An orange-and-black butterfly resting on a green leaf](/blog/usa-2014/20141020-IMG_1272.jpg)

My first (and only) attempt at photographing insects. 

![A close-up of a deer's face and antlers in dappled sunlight](/blog/usa-2014/20141025-IMG_1326.jpg)

I saw a deer while wandering close to our hotel in Yosemite national park. I was able to slowly walk very close to the animal to take a few shots.

![Yosemite Valley's granite cliffs and forest beneath a clear blue sky](/blog/usa-2014/20141025-IMG_1472.jpg)

Yosemite Valley. Feels like a sea of pine trees and big rocks standing out. 

![A deer standing on grass in front of golden autumn shrubs](/blog/usa-2014/20141026-IMG_1779.jpg)

Another deer, smaller this time. Had to walk very slowly to get a good shot as the animal was staring at me. Shot with a 40mm lens so had no zoom to help me out.

![Window light falling across a table and stove in a rustic room](/blog/usa-2014/20141027-IMG_1833.jpg)

Cody (Wyoming) ghost town.

![A barefoot skateboarder attempting a trick in a sunlit skatepark](/blog/usa-2014/28112014-IMG_1936.jpg)

I really like this picture too, a barefoot skateboarder in Los Angeles struggling with his board. 

![The Golden Gate Bridge across the bay with waves washing onto the beach](/blog/usa-2014/14122014-IMG_2213.jpg)

One of my favorite pictures too. I did not have an ND filter nor a tripod for this one, so I used a piece of wood to put the camera on.
